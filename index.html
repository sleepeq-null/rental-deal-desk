          Columns: finished value. Rows: build cost per square foot.

          Draw schedule

          Your equity covers the lot and early costs first. After that the lender funds monthly draws, and interest is added to the loan balance.

          Assumptions: the lot, plans and permits are paid at closing; hard costs, contingency and other soft costs are spent evenly over the build; the loan is a percentage of costs before financing; interest is added to the loan balance until it is paid off at sale or refinance; no rental income comes in during the lease-up months. Taxes on income and sale are not included. This is an underwriting aid, not financial advice.
